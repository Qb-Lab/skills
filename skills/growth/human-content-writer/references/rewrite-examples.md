# Rewrite examples

Each pair is the default draft and the version a person would post. Products are made up;
numbers are illustrative, and in real output every one of them needs a source. Do not copy
these sentences. Copy the moves listed under each pair.

## LinkedIn, founder mode (an invoice-extraction SaaS)

Before:

> [rocket] Excited to share that we're revolutionizing how finance teams handle invoices! In
> today's fast-paced world, manual data entry should be a thing of the past. Our AI-powered
> platform unlocks efficiency, accuracy, and peace of mind. Whether you're a startup or an
> enterprise, we've got you covered. Ready to transform your workflow? [pointing down]

After:

> We spent three weeks on one supplier's invoices.
>
> They print the total in the footer, in a different font, sometimes on page two. We kept
> reading the subtotal instead.
>
> The fix wasn't a better model. We now add up the line items and flag the invoice when the
> total disagrees. Boring. It catches more errors than anything else we've built.
>
> If your AP team has a supplier like this, I'd like to see the invoice. Redact whatever
> you need to.

Moves: opens on the event, not the announcement. One story. A detail only this team would
know (the footer, page two). Admits the fix is boring. The ask is small and specific.

## X, opinionated mode (a budgeting app)

Before:

> Budgeting doesn't have to be hard! Here are 3 reasons our app is a game-changer:
> [check] Simple [check] Smart [check] Secure. Try it today! #fintech #budgeting #money

After:

> Budgeting apps die in week two. That's the Sunday you're supposed to categorise 60
> transactions.
>
> We deleted categories. You get one number: what's safe to spend until payday.

Moves: a claim someone can disagree with. A concrete moment (Sunday, 60 transactions)
instead of three adjectives. No hashtags, no "try it today"; the profile does that job.

## Reddit, founder mode (a puzzle game, posted in a puzzle community)

Before:

> Hey everyone! We're thrilled to announce our innovative new puzzle game that will
> challenge your mind like never before! Download now and let us know what you think!

After:

> **I made a logic puzzle game where no level needs guessing. Trying to find out if the
> difficulty curve is broken.**
>
> Solo dev, so yes, this is my game. I got annoyed at puzzle apps where level 40 quietly
> turns into trial and error, so I wrote a solver that rejects any level it can't finish
> with forced moves only. 120 levels so far.
>
> What I can't tell is whether the ramp works for anyone who isn't me. If you play a few and
> one feels unfair, give me the level number and I'll go look at the solver trace.
>
> Free, no ads in the first 50 levels. I'll put the link in a comment if that's allowed
> here. Mods, remove this if it isn't.

Moves: discloses the affiliation in the first line of the body. Gives the community
something to do that uses their expertise. Explains the interesting part (the solver).
Defers to the rules out loud. No superlatives; Reddit punishes them.

## TikTok caption (the budgeting app; the video shows the spend number turning red)

Before:

> Unlock financial freedom with our amazing app! [money bag][sparkles] Link in bio!
> #money #finance #budget #fyp #viral #savings #moneytok

After:

> the 19th. every month. the 19th.

Moves: the caption finishes the video instead of describing it. It is what the viewer is
already thinking. This account writes lowercase captions, so this one does too. At most one
or two tags that name the niche, and "link in bio" only on the posts that are asking for
the install.

## Instagram caption, educational mode (a carousel on a payday routine)

Before:

> 5 tips to supercharge your savings journey! [flexed arm] Which one is your favorite? Save
> this post for later! [bookmark]

After:

> Slide 4 is the one people argue with.
>
> Rent first. Then the boring bills. Then the number you're actually allowed to spend. Most
> people do it the other way round and wonder where it went.
>
> Save it for payday.

Moves: points at a specific slide, which sends people to swipe. Says the content, not that
there is content. The save ask has a reason and a time.

## Short email, professional mode (the invoice SaaS, to a trial user who stalled)

Before:

> Subject: Unlock the full power of your trial!
>
> Hi there, we hope you're enjoying your experience so far! We wanted to reach out and
> remind you of all the amazing features waiting for you...

After:

> Subject: your 14 invoices
>
> Hi Dana,
>
> You uploaded 14 invoices on Tuesday and haven't exported any. Usually that means the
> export columns don't match your accounting setup.
>
> If that's it, reply with a screenshot of the columns you need and I'll map them for you
> today.
>
> Sam

Moves: the subject is about them. The body is built on what they actually did, so it can
only be written when the usage data is real; without it, do not fake the specificity. One
offer, one way to accept it, signed by a person.

## What the rewrites have in common

- The first line is the most specific line. Announcements, greetings and scene-setting are
  cut, not shortened.
- One idea. The second idea is the next post.
- Nouns a camera could film (footer, Sunday, level 40) in place of abstractions
  (efficiency, journey, experience).
- Rhythm is uneven because thought is. A short sentence after two long ones. A fragment
  where it lands.
- The ending is an action, a real question, or nothing. Never a moral, a slogan or "what do
  you think?" asked of nobody in particular.
- Nothing in it could be pasted under a competitor's logo.

## Over-correcting

Human does not mean sloppy. Do not add typos, filler ("ngl", "honestly"), or slang the
voice does not already use; that is a different costume, and readers spot it just as fast.
Lowercase, fragments and jokes appear only when the project's own samples show them. When
in doubt, write it plainer, not quirkier.
