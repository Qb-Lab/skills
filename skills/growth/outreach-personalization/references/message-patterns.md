# Message patterns

Short beats clever. Every pattern below is a shape, not a template to fill blindly: if the
research does not support a line, cut the line.

## Cold email (day 0), under 110 words before signature

```
Subject: <their thing> at <company>        (under seven words, no clickbait)

Hi <first name>,

<Observation: one or two sentences, a verified fact from research. Specific enough that
they know a person looked.>

<Bridge: one sentence that connects the fact to a common operational cost, as a
possibility. "When enquiries arrive on two channels, someone usually ends up moving them
by hand." Not "your team wastes hours".>

<What the agency does, one sentence, concrete: what gets built and what changes for them.>

<Ask, low friction: "Worth a 20-minute call to see if it applies?" or "Want a one-page
outline first?">

<opt-out line>

<signature>
```

## First follow-up (business day 4), under 80 words

Same thread. Give something: a one-line idea specific to them, or a question that shows
thought. Never "just following up" or "bumping this".

```
Hi <first name>,

<One useful thing: "One option that tends to work for brokerages your size is routing
portal leads straight into the CRM by area, so nobody has to forward them.">

<Restate the ask in one line.>

<opt-out line>
<name>
```

## Final follow-up (about a week later), under 60 words

Close the loop politely. No guilt, no fake deadline.

```
Hi <first name>,

I'll leave this here so I don't crowd your inbox. If routing enquiries becomes a priority,
reply any time and I'll send a short outline.

<opt-out line>
<name>
```

## LinkedIn connection request, under 300 characters

The observation and who you are. No ask, no link.

```
Hi <first name>, saw <company> is <verified fact>. I build internal tools and automations
for <industry> teams in the UAE. Would be good to connect.
```

## LinkedIn message after accepting, under 80 words

The cold email without subject, opt-out line or signature block.

## Words that make a message sound generated

Cut on sight: "I hope this finds you well", "I came across", "quick question", "touch
base", "leverage", "seamless", "streamline your operations" as a whole sentence,
"game-changer", "in today's fast-paced", any compliment that is not about a specific
thing they did, and any sentence that would fit any company.

## Angles to vary (record as `variant`)

- `observation-routing`: their enquiry flow (forms, WhatsApp, portals).
- `observation-hiring`: a role they are hiring for that signals manual work.
- `observation-expansion`: a new office, project or market.
- `pilot-outline`: lead with the one-page pilot outline offer.

Change one thing at a time between variants so the weekly review can tell what worked.
