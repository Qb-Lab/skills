# Offers

Two plans. No prices in public copy. Every plan card says: **"Get a fixed quote on the
call."** The owner commits to quoting live within the 30 minutes.

## Subscription (primary, "Most clients choose this")

A senior product team on a monthly plan. QBLab builds the product, then stays on for new
features, enhancements, updates, monitoring and looking after the tech.

- One active request at a time
- Most requests delivered in 2–5 days
- Queue as many requests as you like
- Pause or cancel anytime
- The client owns the code and the accounts; the product keeps running on their
  infrastructure

On cancel: anything in progress is finished and handed over, the client keeps the code and
the accounts, the product stays live, and they can come back whenever they have more to
build.

Lead with this plan. Position the project plan as the way in for people who want a single
delivery first, with the subscription as the natural next step.

## Project (secondary)

Fixed scope, fixed quote, one delivery.

- Scope agreed on the call
- One fixed quote, no hourly billing
- Delivered, deployed and handed over
- Move to a subscription afterwards to keep shipping

## The process (as shown on the site)

| When | Step | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 1 | A 30-minute call | Client describes the product. QBLab asks the questions that change the estimate. Client leaves with a plan and a fixed quote. |
| Week 1 | Plan and design | Scope, screens and data model agreed. The client sees what will be built before anything is built. |
| Weeks 2–3 | Build | Working software every few days, not a reveal at the end. AI does the fast part; a senior engineer reviews every change. |
| Week 4 | Launch | Deployed on the client's accounts and infrastructure, with stores and domains pointed at it. |
| Ongoing | Keep shipping | Month by month: features, updates, monitoring. Pause or stop whenever. |

## AI accounts

For building, QBLab uses its own tools. If the product itself has AI features, they run on
accounts the client owns, so the client controls cost and data.

## After launch

Most clients stay on a subscription. If they take it in-house, QBLab hands over
documentation and walks their team through it.

## The conversion event

Every call to action is the literal string **"Book a free 30-min call"**, sub-line **"Leave
with a plan and a fixed quote."** Booking runs through Cal.com. Cal's form asks project type
and budget. There is no contact form on the site; WhatsApp is a plain text link.
