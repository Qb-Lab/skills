# Positioning

Source of truth: qblab.co (`content/site.ts`) and its architecture doc. Do not restate this
page from memory; read it.

## One line

**QBLab builds complete products in weeks, not months.**

## What QBLab is

A small senior product team based in Dubai, UAE, working worldwide. QBLab designs, builds,
deploys and maintains complete products: the web app, the mobile app (iOS and Android), and
the backend behind them. One team owns the whole thing from first call to launch and after.

Site description, verbatim:

> QBLab designs, builds and runs complete products — web apps, mobile apps and the backend
> behind them — in weeks, not months. AI-assisted development is why it's fast; senior
> engineering review is why it's safe.

## The promise and the mechanism

- **Promise: speed.** "Your product, live in weeks. Not months." A first version follows a
  four-week shape: plan and design in week one, build in weeks two and three, launch in week
  four. On a subscription, most individual requests ship in two to five days.
- **Mechanism: AI-assisted development.** This is the reason it is fast. It is stated as a
  reason, not as a feature to sell.
- **Guarantee: senior engineering review.** A senior engineer reviews every change before it
  ships. This is why it is safe. Always paired with the speed claim; never omitted.

## Audience

1. **Founders and business owners** first. They have a product to build, want it live this
   quarter, and want one team to handle everything.
2. **Technical leads** second. They evaluate the stack, the review process, and ownership.

Language is written for the first group. The second group gets their answers in the FAQ, the
stack list, and on the call.

## What QBLab builds

Web apps · Mobile apps (iOS and Android from one codebase) · Backend and APIs · AI features
(assistants, search, automation, where they earn their place) · Dashboards and internal
tools · Integrations (payments, auth, messaging, third-party services).

Stack named on the site: TypeScript, React, Next.js, Flutter, Node.js, NestJS, Express,
Python, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Prisma, Tailwind CSS, Docker, Google Cloud.

## Ownership stance

The client owns the code and the accounts from day one. Everything is built in repositories
and cloud accounts that belong to them. If they stop working with QBLab, nothing changes:
the product keeps running on their infrastructure. Say this early and often; it removes the
biggest fear a founder has about an outside team.

## What QBLab is not

Not a body shop, not an hourly-rate vendor, not a large on-site team, not a certified vendor
for regulated procurement, and not a partner for finding scope by building for a year.

## Location

"Based in Dubai, UAE, working worldwide." Stated once as trust, never as a ranking keyword.
QBLab is not based in Lebanon; `qblab-lb` is only a repository name.
