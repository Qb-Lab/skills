# Post patterns

Three shapes, one source. Every variant must survive the test: would a founder who has
never heard of QBLab stop scrolling, and would they know what QBLab does by the end?

## A. Build log ("shipped in N days")

The default and usually the winner. Shows the thing and the elapsed time.

```
<What shipped>, live in <N> days.

<Who it is for, one line.>

What went in:
- <feature a client would notice>
- <feature>
- <feature>

<One line on how: AI did the fast part, a senior engineer reviewed every change.>

<One line on ownership or what happens next.>

<Invite: "If you have a product that needs to be live this quarter, the call is free: <link>">
```

Rules: features are user-visible, elapsed days come from commit dates, no adjectives in the
hook.

## B. Lesson ("the thing we got wrong / right")

One concrete decision from the work, what it cost or saved, and the rule you now follow.

```
<The rule, stated plainly, twelve words or fewer.>

<What happened, three or four short lines, specific.>

<What it cost or saved, in days if you can prove it.>

<The rule again, as advice to a founder.>

<Question to the reader, or invite.>
```

Rules: one lesson only; the story must be real and cleared for sharing; no humblebrag.

## C. Founder question ("if you are building X")

Speaks to the reader's situation, ends in a question that makes them reply or DM.

```
<A situation the reader recognises, as a question or a "you" statement.>

<Why it happens, two lines.>

<What QBLab did about it once, one line, generic if not cleared.>

<The question: what are you doing about it? / what is blocking you?>
```

Rules: the reader is the subject, not QBLab; the only proof is one short example.

## Anti-patterns

- A hook that is an announcement ("Thrilled to share…", "Big news…")
- A wall of hashtags, more than one emoji, or emoji as bullets
- "AI-powered" as the subject of the post
- Any number that did not come from commit dates or `proof.md`
- Naming a client or product not cleared in the overlay
- Ending with a slogan instead of an invite or a question
