# Content buckets

A bucket is the job a piece of content does. Pick the job first, then the idea. Each bucket
names the evidence it cannot exist without; when that evidence is missing the bucket is
unavailable, and the output says what would unlock it.

Funnel jobs: **awareness** (a stranger stops and learns the product exists), **engagement**
(they reply, save, share or follow), **trust** (they believe the claim), **conversion**
(they install, sign up, book or buy).

| Bucket | Job | Use when | Evidence it requires | Shape |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product demonstration | awareness, conversion | the product shows a visible result in under 30 seconds | a real screen recording or the product in hand | open on the result, then the three taps that got there |
| Pain / problem | awareness | the audience feels the problem but has not looked for a fix | the pain in the audience's own words, with a source | name the moment it hurts, exactly; product appears late or not at all |
| Education | trust, engagement | the buyer needs to understand something before they can want the product | something the team knows from doing the work | one technique, shown once, usable without the product |
| Founder / build in public | trust | early stage; the founder is visible; the audience follows builders | a real decision, number or mistake the owner will share | what we decided this week, what it cost, what happened |
| Opinion / contrarian | awareness, engagement | the team disagrees with how the category does something | a position the product actually embodies | the common belief, why it fails, what we do instead |
| Comparison | conversion | buyers are choosing between named alternatives | checkable facts about the alternative, with URLs | same task, both ways, side by side; no insults |
| Transformation | awareness, trust | there is a clear before and after | a real before and a real after (the owner's own counts) | the before state, one cut, the after state |
| Customer story | trust, conversion | a real customer exists and agreed to be mentioned | owner-supplied story and permission | their situation, what they tried, what changed, in their words |
| Community question | engagement | the account has some audience and wants replies and research | a question the team does not know the answer to | one specific question with a stake ("we're deciding X this week") |
| Entertaining | awareness | the audience is on a feed for fun, not for solutions | a true, recognisable situation from the audience's life | the situation played straight; the product is a prop, not the punchline |
| Meme / culture | awareness, engagement | humor is allowed and the format is current on that platform | a format seen live this month, not remembered | the format unchanged, the caption specific to the niche |
| Trend response | awareness | a dated radar entry shows a movement the product has a real stake in | the radar entry with its URL and date | the news in one line, then the take only this team can give |
| Behind the scenes | trust | the making is interesting: craft, hardware, art, support, data | real footage, screenshots or artefacts of the work | one artefact, shown raw, with what it taught |
| Proof | trust, conversion | real numbers, reviews or usage exist | numbers with source and date; reviews that are real and quotable | the number, how it was measured, what it does not show |
| Launch | awareness, conversion | something ships on a date | the thing, working, and the date | what it is, who it is for, the one thing to do now |
| Objection handling | conversion | the same doubt keeps coming up | the objection verbatim (reviews, sales calls, comments) | state the doubt fairly, answer with a fact or a demo, concede what is true |

## Starting mix by objective

Shares of a batch, before availability cuts. Adjust with `learnings.md`: a bucket with
evidence for it gets more, one with evidence against it gets less, and the output says so.

| Objective | Lead with (about half) | Support (about a third) | A little of |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Users or installs (consumer) | product demonstration, transformation, entertaining | pain, meme or trend response | proof, objection handling |
| Wishlists, players or streamer attention (game) | product demonstration (gameplay is the content), behind the scenes, founder | community question, entertaining, transformation (before and after of a level or the art) | proof, launch |
| Leads or first customers (B2B) | pain, education, founder | comparison, objection handling, opinion | product demonstration, customer story |
| Revenue from existing traffic | objection handling, comparison, proof | customer story, product demonstration | education |
| Audience or followers | opinion, education, founder | community question, behind the scenes | product demonstration |
| Traffic (search, referral) | education, comparison | pain, proof | opinion |
| A launch in the next 30 days | launch, product demonstration, behind the scenes | founder, objection handling | community question |

Stage adjusts the mix: before launch there is nothing to prove, so founder, behind the
scenes, pain and community question carry it; with early traction, proof and customer story
become available and should displace education first.

## Rules

- One bucket per idea. An idea that claims three jobs does none.
- No batch is all one job. Even a conversion push keeps one or two ideas that earn attention
  from strangers; even an awareness push keeps one that asks for the install or the signup.
- Product demonstration is available to almost every project and is the most underused
  bucket. If the batch has none, justify it.
- Customer story, proof and comparison are the buckets where invention is most tempting.
  Without the evidence they are listed as blocked on input, never written around.
